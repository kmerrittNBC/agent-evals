**What can actually be compared?** Vals AI’s **83.15% Sol versus 73.03% Luna** is a reasonably like-for-like Terminal-Bench 2.1 comparison within *Vals’* Terminus 2 setup. Artificial Analysis’s **69% Sol versus 64% Luna** is likewise a comparison within its **Codex/DeepSWE v1.1** setup. DeepSeek’s scaffold table supports **within-DeepSeek** comparisons of agent harnesses under its stated controls.

The other cross-row rankings are **reported outcomes under differing setups**, not controlled head-to-head model rankings. In particular, DeepSeek’s 90.6%, Z.ai’s 88.2%, Qwen’s 73.0%, and Vals’ GPT-6 figures use different terminal agents and sometimes different budgets or network rules; DeepSWE’s mini-SWE, Claude Code, OpenCode and Codex results should not be merged into one league table. Artificial Analysis also [replaced Terminal-Bench 2.1 with 4.0](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/coding-agents-benchmarking) in its current Coding Agent Index: a 4.0 result is **not** a missing 2.1 score.
