## What the disagreements do—and don’t—show

- **Terminal-Bench results are not interchangeable across harnesses.** For example, GLM-5.3 is 88.2% in Z.ai’s Claude Code evaluation but 83.4% in Fidian’s Terminus 2 run. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is 90.6% in DeepSeek’s Minimal harness but 72.4% in Fidian’s Terminus 2 run. Those gaps do not establish which model is better: the harness, provider route, and settings differ.
- **Fidian supports a limited like-for-like comparison** among the models it ran with Terminus 2: GPT-6 Sol 88.0%, GPT-6 Luna 79.8%, GLM-5.3 83.4%, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 72.4% on TB 2.1. That is evidence about **those model-plus-provider configurations on Fidian’s setup**, not a universal ranking. Fidian’s table does not include Qwen3.8-27B among the reported rows above.
- **Luna’s TB 2.1 results disagree:** Fidian reports 79.8%, Vals 73.03%. Both describe Terminus 2-based TB 2.1 evaluations, but the available details do not establish identical run conditions or explain the difference. Do not average them or treat either as a definitive model-only score.
- **DeepSWE supports even fewer direct cross-model comparisons.** Qwen used Claude Code; Z.ai reports mini-swe-agent; DeepSeek provides results for both mini-swe-agent and its own Minimal harness; OpenAI does not disclose the harness for its Sol and Luna scores. GLM’s 66.9% model-card score and the leaderboard’s rounded ~69% ±3% are separately attributed mini-swe-agent reports, but the published information is insufficient to reconcile them precisely.
- DeepSeek’s own scaffold comparison is useful evidence of **harness sensitivity**: for example, its TB 2.1 result is 90.3% with mini-swe-agent versus 90.6% with DeepSeek Harness Minimal; for DeepSWE it reports 74.2% with mini-swe-agent versus 72.6% with Minimal. These are within-source comparisons, but they do not make results from other providers’ evaluation setups directly comparable.
- Do not mix up **GPT-6 Sol** with **GPT-5.6 Sol**, **Qwen3.8-27B** with **Qwen3.8-Max**, or **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash** with another DeepSeek V4 variant. Similarly, Terminal-Bench 2.1 and DeepSWE v1.1 measure different task sets, so their percentages should not be compared to each other.
