## What comparisons the evidence supports

- **Terminal-Bench:** Vals provides scores for all five models under the same named Terminus 2 harness and Terminal-Bench 2.1 variant. That supports a **within-Vals comparison of those evaluated configurations**, but not a universal model ranking: effort settings and repeat counts differ, and the other reported scores show large harness-related variation. Mercor also supports a narrower comparison between **GPT-6 Sol (85.4%) and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (86.5%)** under Mercor’s leaderboard, though Mercor does not disclose enough run detail to reproduce it.
- **DeepSWE:** Mercor reports four of the five requested models under mini-swe-agent with common stated limits: Sol 70.5%, Luna 67.0%, GLM-5.3 70.5%, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 71.7%. Those are the closest common-setup comparison for those four. Their reported margins overlap, so the evidence does **not** establish a reliable ordering among them. Qwen3.8-27B is missing from this Mercor comparison.
- Fireworks provides another shared **avg@3** comparison for Sol, Luna, GLM-5.3, and DeepSeek, but its DeepSeek results distinguish the base **V4.1 Flash** from **V4.1 Flash (1017)**, and the benchmark page does not identify a specific harness snapshot.
- The vendor/model-card numbers are **not one common experiment**. For example, the model cards report Terminal-Bench 2.1 results of 90.6 for DeepSeek and 88.2 for GLM, while Vals reports 74.53 and 71.54 for those models under Terminus 2. The reported scores establish that the sources made those claims; they do not justify ranking across different harnesses and configurations.
- Terminal-Bench 2.1 and DeepSWE v1.1 measure different task sets and use different scoring setups. Their percentages should not be compared to each other as if they were one scale.

**Unverified score cells: none found.** Some settings—especially the harness details behind OpenAI’s DeepSWE results and several leaderboard-only results—remain undisclosed, so those scores are sourced but not fully reproducible from the public reporting.
