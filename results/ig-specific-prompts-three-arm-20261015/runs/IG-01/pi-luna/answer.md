## What the evidence supports

- **The Terminal-Bench numbers do not support one simple cross-source ranking.** Each model has at least one score, but the runs mix evaluators and harnesses. Where results disagree, the separate figures above should remain separate: for example, GPT-6 Sol is 83.15% on Vals versus 87.7% on Fidian; GLM-5.3 is 71.54% on Vals, 83.4% on Fidian and 88.2% in Z.ai’s report. These are model-plus-evaluator results, not evidence of one uniquely correct score.
- **Some within-evaluator comparisons are more informative.** Vals reports Terminus 2 results for all five requested models, so its scores are the closest common-set comparison in this roundup. Even there, they are not a fully controlled comparison of reasoning effort, provider or other model-specific settings. Fidian also gives same-evaluator results for GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna and GLM-5.3, but uses its own Terminus-2/Daytona setup.
- **DeepSWE’s common-harness comparison is strongest for GLM-5.3.** Its Z.ai-reported mini-swe-agent score (66.9%) and Datacurve’s mini-swe-agent result (68.96% ± 3.02) are close, with overlapping uncertainty. The other DeepSWE scores come from model-provider reports with incomplete or different harness disclosures: in particular, Qwen reports Claude Code, while DeepSeek reports mini-swe-agent. OpenAI’s reported scores do not specify enough harness detail in the cited disclosure to establish like-for-like comparability.
- **Do not compare Terminal-Bench 2.1 percentages directly with DeepSWE v1.1 percentages.** They are different benchmarks with different task suites and evaluation setups. The Terminal-Bench release describes an 89-task variant; DeepSWE v1.1 uses 113 tasks. ([Terminal-Bench 2.1](https://www.tbench.ai/news/terminal-bench-2-1); [DeepSWE](https://deepswe.datacurve.ai/))
- **A controlled head-to-head would require rerunning the exact model variants with the same benchmark version, harness, settings, sandbox and trial protocol.** In particular, DeepSeek’s reported three samples per Terminal-Bench task should not be assumed to match the benchmark repository’s standard submission protocol.
