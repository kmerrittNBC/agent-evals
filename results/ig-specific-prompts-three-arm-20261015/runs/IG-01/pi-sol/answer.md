**What the evidence supports:** Within **Vals’ TB-2.1/Terminus 2 board**, the reported order is Sol 83.15 > DeepSeek 74.53 > Luna 73.03 > GLM-5.3 71.54 > Qwen3.8-27B 58.43. Within **Fidian’s TB-2.1 run**, Sol 87.7 > GLM-5.3 83.4 > Luna 79.7 > DeepSeek 72.4; it provides no verified 27B entry. The changed ordering is a reason *not* to assemble a ranking from the highest score reported for each model.

For **DeepSWE v1.1**, Mercor supplies the closest shared-run comparison for four of the five: DeepSeek 71.7, Sol and GLM-5.3 both 70.5, and Luna 67.0, with the reported uncertainty ranges above. It does **not** establish that those small differences are meaningful, nor does it include a verified Qwen3.8-27B score. OpenAI’s, Qwen’s, Z.ai’s and DeepSeek’s vendor figures are useful **attributed results**, not one controlled five-model test. In particular, neither *Terminal-Bench 2.1* nor *DeepSWE v1.1* should be confused with Fidian’s separate **TB-fn** variant or with scores for Qwen3.8-Max or GLM-5.3 Flash.
