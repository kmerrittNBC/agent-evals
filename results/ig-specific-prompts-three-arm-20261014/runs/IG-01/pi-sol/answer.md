**What can be compared:** Vals’ five TB 2.1 entries are the clearest *within-evaluator, same-named-harness* comparison: **Sol 83.15 > V4.1 Flash 74.53 > Luna 73.03 > GLM-5.3 71.54 > Qwen3.8-27B 58.43** in that setup. OpenAI’s own DeepSWE report supports **Sol 68.8 versus Luna 66.6** under its reported max-effort evaluation, but does not document enough harness detail to equate that comparison with Mercor’s. DeepSeek’s scaffold table directly demonstrates why harness matching matters: **90.6 versus 90.3 on the same TB 2.1 variant** with two different scaffolds. Small DeepSWE differences across OpenAI, Z.ai, DeepSeek, Datacurve and Mercor **do not establish a cross-model ranking**: evaluator, effort, limits and repetitions differ.
